Skin AI

A dermatologist-grade
read-out in 2 seconds.

Ankit samples skin pixels from anatomically-correct cheek and forehead regions defined by the face mesh, then computes six clinical-style metrics. The output powers product recommendations — your client sees their skin type, you see a lead.

01
ITA° skin tone classification
Categorises into 6 Fitzpatrick-adjacent groups for shade matching.
02
Evenness & texture
Local variance across cheek samples estimates texture uniformity.
03
Redness & under-eye index
A* channel deviation + periorbital luminance for dark-circle score.
