Workflow

Three steps. Zero friction.

01
Open & allow camera
Customer taps "Launch Studio". Browser asks for camera permission. Everything runs locally on-device — no video leaves the phone.
02
Pick & tune filters
Swipe through hairstyle, beard, lipstick, eye, blush, foundation, glam filters. Adjust intensity with a slider. See it live instantly.
03
Capture & share
Snap a photo, compare before/after, download or share with the salon. Or run a full skin analysis report.
